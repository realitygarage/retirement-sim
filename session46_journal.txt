--- v5.17.1: SALE-PROCEEDS CARD -- 72e1101 ---

- engine.js aggregateMonthlyToPeriod: settleDraw, wfMtgPaydown, wfDebtPaid,
  wfReserveFill, wfToSavings now SUM every pool month in the period (were
  poolRow-only). Display fields only -- nothing reads them for balances.
- engine.js: new poolDebt on monthly rows and annual rows -- per debt
  {key,label,rate,eligible,paid,carried}, avalanche order (rate desc): the
  three HI debts (always listed, flagged when not closing-eligible) plus
  every closing-eligible loan. Annual: paid sums every pass, carried is the
  last pass's post-payoff balance.
- App.jsx Cash-Flow Engine section: one card per pool year (every sale year
  + the obligation's year). Title "6th St Sale (Dec 2026): Where the
  Proceeds Go" (same-year sales joined with " + ", each gets its own Net
  proceeds row); obligation-only card "One-Time Obligation (Apr 2026): Not
  Funded by a Sale". Rows: Net proceeds, Obligation, One-time draw (at
  sale), Mortgage principal, one row per debt paid (with rate), Reserve/
  buffer, Savings, Remaining (amber if off by >$2), then "Debt still carried
  after sale" + Total carried. Full dollars, mono, right-aligned; title 13px
  bold, rows 11.5px, bordered card. $0 rows dimmed (55% opacity); the carried
  section uses `muted` at full opacity instead (Bob: the dimmed carried rows
  were too low-contrast).
- Bob's rule confirmed (already how the engine works): net proceeds ->
  obligation -> one-time draw -> [mortgage principal, if set] -> HI debt +
  closing-eligible loans by rate, highest first -> reserve/buffer -> savings.
  A draw that squeezes the payoff leaves the rest carried (e.g. a $150K draw:
  CC paid in full, Nolan partly (-$78,314, $68,909 carried), Sophia
  untouched ($53,156 carried)).
- Clarified for Bob: the card's draw is the "One-time draw ($, at sale)"
  field, NOT Lifestyle Draws (Draw 1 etc.), which pull from invested
  savings and never touch sale proceeds. Row renamed accordingly.
- Tests: E5/L1 version strings. R10 getByText('One-Time Obligation') now
  exact (the obligation-only card title also contains it). K3's regex
  /\$(\d+\.?\d*)M/ matched the card's "$0" + "Mortgage principal" run
  together by textContent ("$0M") -- now requires a decimal. R10/E5/L1/K3
  (+B9/D4/F1 caught by the -g pattern) 7/7; Groups R + V 44/44.
