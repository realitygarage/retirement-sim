--- PART B: ROOT CAUSE (corrected the handoff's own diagnosis) ---

The handoff's diagnosis assumed a two-engine drift (annual buildScenario vs.
monthly wfData) -- stale, per the same v5.0.0 single-engine collapse noted
above. Both the Simulator tab and Cash Flow tab read the same
buildMonthlyScenario output now, so there was never a cross-engine bug here.

Actual root cause, traced by hand through the Hermite spline math:
engine.js samples the curve as workFromCurve(mo/12, p.workPts), where mo=0 is
BASE.startMonth (July) -- the curve's zero point is anchored to July 1. But
WorkCurveEditor's composeYr (App.jsx) converted a user-picked (calendar
year, quarter) into a point's yr field as `wholeYr + (q-1)/4` -- anchored to
January 1 of that calendar year. The two zero-points only agree when
BASE.startMonth===1; with the real default of July, every point except the
hard-locked point 0 landed ~0.5yr (2 quarters) later than its on-screen
label. A point labeled "2027 Q1" was actually feeding the engine the
fractional position of July 2027. The Work Income Curve chart on the
Simulator tab "looked right" because it's drawn by replotting workFromCurve
using that same mislabeled yr -- it was self-consistently wrong, not an
independent check.

Fix: rewrote wholeYrOf/qOf/composeYr to convert in absolute-month space,
anchored at BASE.startYear/startMonth (the same way buildMonthlyScenario
derives mo from calYear/calMonth) instead of Jan 1. Confined entirely to
WorkCurveEditor -- no engine.js change, no SAVE_SCHEMA_VERSION bump (existing
saved workPts numeric values are untouched; only their calendar-label
display corrects itself -- verified a default point like {yr:2,...} now
correctly displays "2028 Q3" instead of the old mislabeled "2028 Q1").
